The story

How it all started

I still remember the day Djappa Man reached out to us. For those who don't know, this man is a legend. He's the founder of ZOMRA — the crew that literally built the foundation of underground hip-hop in Tunisia. Anti-system, raw, real — that's ZOMRA. That's Djappa Man.

But here's the thing: he had been gone for a long time. Years away from the scene. No rap, no art, completely silent. When someone like that disappears, you feel it in the culture.

Then out of nowhere, he hit us up at WYRM. "I'm back," he told us. "I want to create something new. I need a complete branding — something personal, something that represents who I am now."

I won't lie — I was honored. Nervous too. This is Djappa Man we're talking about. The guy who pioneered the movement. And he chose us to bring his vision back to life. He trusted us with his legacy. This wasn't just another project. This was history in the making.

The Challenge

After years away from the scene, Djappa Man needed a complete visual rebrand that honored his legendary status in Tunisian hip-hop while representing his evolution as an artist. The brand had to work across podcast series, music releases, social media and live streams — without losing the raw, authentic energy that defined ZOMRA.

The Solution

A complete identity system: a character that captures his essence, Mental Files podcast branding with an animated intro, music cover designs, YouTube thumbnails, and a cohesive visual language bridging his underground roots with his current multimedia vision.

D

OUTCOME

A legend, rebranded — and still rolling.

A complete visual identity running across the Mental Files podcast, every music release and the full social presence — an ongoing collaboration from 2023 to today, bridging the ZOMRA heritage with Djappa Man's new era.
