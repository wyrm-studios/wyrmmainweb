About

The gold from nature

AUREA is Tunisian, founded in 2025 by Amine and Hadil. The problem it solves: luxury fashion that combines modern and old-money style — wearable and affordable for the young, modern generation, for the rich and not rich alike, in high-quality materials sourced from different countries: Tunisia, Turkey, Thailand, Italy, France — a supply chain that symbolises the gold from nature.

The name carries the story: l'or est un métal précieux, de couleur jaune brillante, très malléable, inoxydable et rare. Gold has fascinated humanity since prehistory — found pure in rivers, worked at scale by the ancient Egyptians around 3000 BC, present in the oldest known goldwork from Varna around 4500 BC. AUREA borrows that permanence: rare, untarnished, brilliant.

Logo

A sunrise, set in serif

The mark pairs a rising sun with radiating rays above the AUREA wordmark — set in an elegant serif with a distinctive swooping R. The logo must always be used in its entirety, never altered in shape, color or proportions.

Typography

Two faces, one voice

Montserrat carries headlines and paragraphs; Playfair Display sets the subheadings. Used in their original, approved versions, balanced to reflect the look and feel of the brand.

Imagery

The launch campaign

Shot on the shore: rust, cream and ink against water and stone. The imagery rules are simple — natural light, honest textures, and silhouettes that read as luxury without shouting.

Applications

The brand, out in the world

From hang tags to signage to the digital storefront — one sunrise, every touchpoint.

The Challenge

"Old money" is easy to draw and hard to wear — most luxury identities either whisper into invisibility or cosplay wealth. AUREA needed a system that reads luxury at first glance yet stays young, wearable and affordable — without tipping into costume.

The Solution

A complete guidelines book: the sunrise-serif lockup with strict usage rules, Montserrat × Playfair Display pairing, the ink / cream / gold palette, and a full shore-shot imagery direction — one warm, gilded language from hang tag to storefront to screen.
