WDRINK — Complete Brand Identity & Motion Design ⚡
We built the complete visual identity for WDRINK — a vibrant energy drink brand that stands out from the crowd.
What We Created:
Brand Identity: Full visual system with bold, playful typography and energetic color palette
Mascot Design: Custom character design — a fun, animated blue can that brings the brand to life
Packaging Design: Eye-catching can design for Red Cherry flavor (250 ML) with "Energize • Focus • Create" messaging
Motion Design: Dynamic video content that captures the brand's high-energy spirit with the "RUN AS FAST AS YOU CAN" concept
This project showcases our ability to build brands from concept to execution — combining strategy, design, and motion to create memorable brand experiences.
