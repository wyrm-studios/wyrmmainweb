The story

Standing out from the crowd

The energy drink shelf is a wall of noise — every can screaming at the same frequency. WDRINK came to us with a simple ambition: don't just join the shouting, be the one can people reach for because it looks alive.

So we built a brand that moves. Bold, playful typography that never sits still. An electric blue palette charged with one cherry-red accent. And a mascot nobody else on the shelf has: a fun, animated blue can with a face — the brand itself, turned into a character.

From strategy to the can in your hand to the frames of the motion campaign, everything speaks one language: energy you can see.

The Challenge

A new energy drink entering a shelf ruled by global giants — where every competitor owns loud colors, extreme sports and typography that yells. WDRINK needed to be impossible to ignore without looking like anyone else, and needed a brand world flexible enough to stretch from a 250 ML can to a full website.

The Solution

A complete identity with personality at its core: bold playful typography, an electric blue system charged with cherry red, a custom animated can mascot, packaging built around Energize • Focus • Create, a motion campaign under one command — RUN AS FAST AS YOU CAN — and a live website carrying it all to the screen.
