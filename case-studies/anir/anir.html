Who are we

A dynamic company that evolved and thrived

ANIR is a passionate company focused on innovation and committed to making a difference in the world of smart wallets — a product born from a simple observation: the things we carry every day are the least protected things we own.

The risk of theft through emerging technology, and the high likelihood of losing wallets and essential documents — that's the problem ANIR was designed to kill. Redefining the future of smart wallets means security you don't have to think about.

The identity — charte graphique

A monogram with an agenda

The ANIR mark deconstructs the letters into sharp, angular strokes — an N and R fused into one engineered monogram that reads like a circuit trace. Precision-cut, no ornament, all intent.

Signal red on carbon, with bone white space. Click any chip to copy its hex.

The product

Smart solutions for everyday carry

A sleek RFID-protected wallet with a quick-access pull mechanism — 6–7 cards, 4 leather pockets, a secure snap closure and a cash pocket. Carbon-fiber / aluminum composite body with an embedded A9G chip and an NFC shield layer.

Go-to-market

Two phases, one funnel

Phase 1 — 15 days before release: Instagram lead forms, influencer ads and Facebook ads pour traffic into a landing page that feeds the email list. Hype first, hardware second.

Phase 2 — product day: the email and SMS lists get first access through the sales landing page. The audience arrives warm; day one converts.

The Challenge

A hardware startup with a genuinely new product and zero brand presence. ANIR needed an identity that could feel engineered rather than decorated — sharp enough for tech press, credible enough for investors — plus a pitch deck that could carry a hardware story through problem, market, product and financials without losing the room.

The Solution

A complete charte graphique — the angular N/R monogram, signal red / carbon / bone palette, mockups and letterform anatomy — paired with a 22-page investor deck in signal yellow on carbon black: stat counters, competitor tables, cost breakdowns and a two-phase go-to-market. One visual language from logo to ledger.
