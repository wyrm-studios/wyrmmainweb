Overview

The brief

Every studio preaches positioning — few can show their own. The brief for project № 001 was internal but unforgiving: distill five years of work into an identity that signals strategy, design and technology under one roof — and proves it the moment someone lands on the site.

The answer was a system, not a logo. A slogan sharp enough to lead with. A five-color palette with one crimson reserved for action. A wordmark, a signature and a mascot that make the studio feel human. And a motion language that turns every scroll into a brand moment.
